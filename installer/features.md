# VR features

Black & White in SteamVR: you are the god, and the god hand is your own hand.

## Your saves
- Your existing Black & White saves work with the mod, and saves made with the mod still load without it.

## Seeing the world
- The whole island in **stereo 3D with head tracking**: lean in to watch a villager, look up at the sky, look down over the land like a giant.
- **VR from the start**: the game goes into the headset as soon as it starts. The opening, the intro videos and the loading screens show on a screen in front of you.
- **Menus and panels** float on a screen in front of you; point at it and the laser is the mouse.
- **Cutscenes** play on a cinema screen, so the game never moves you around.
- **Captions and hints** sit where you can read them: the spirits' lines, "Pick Up - trigger" by the hand, the tutorial's tips.
- **Sound follows your head**, distant textures don't shimmer, and things are drawn further away than in the flat game.

## Your hand is the god hand
- **Pick up** villagers, rocks, trees and food with your own hand, **throw** at your arm's speed, catch things in the air, and scoop wood or grain from a store, even from high above.
- **Gravity gloves**: point at something out of reach, grip, and flick your hand back to pull it into your hand.
- **Both hands grab**: carry and throw with either hand, and pass things from one hand to the other.
- **Choosing disciples**: carry a villager over a field, a tree or a building and soft motes show where it will land and the job it will take. Let go high in the sky and things fall.
- **Tap** scrolls, signs and doors; a **laser** reaches far away, and can send the hand out and call it back.
- **Your creature**: pet it or slap it with your hand, hold food up for it to take, and lead it on the leash.
- **Miracles**: draw the gestures in the air with your fingertip, then pour the miracle where you want it, or give it to your creature.
- **Building**: carry a scaffold, twist your wrist to turn its blueprint, and let go to build.

## Moving around
- **Fly** with the stick, **turn** in snaps or smoothly, rise and descend, drag the land, or travel to a spot with two quick trigger pulls.
- **Teleport** instead, if you prefer: hold A to aim an energy arc at the land, and let go to be there, with a bird's-eye view like the flat game's.

## Made for comfort
- **Welcome to VR**: a new game starts with a short setup page for turning, teleport, the comfort vignette, your main hand and move speed.
- **VR Options** in the game's pause menu: snap or smooth turning, teleport movement, a **comfort vignette** that darkens the edges of the view while you move, the gravity gloves, the other hand grabbing, speeds, left-handed play, the two-hand grab and throw strength. Your choices are kept through updates.
- **Seated or standing**: Recenter view (in VR Options, or hold the other hand's stick click) makes where you are the view's height and forward.
- **Snapshot button** (the other hand's B): saves pictures and the log when something looks wrong, to send with a bug report.
