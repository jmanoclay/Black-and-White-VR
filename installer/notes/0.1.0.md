The first public release of Black & White VR: the whole game, played in SteamVR with the god hand at your controller. An early version: expect rough edges, and please report what you find.

## Getting started
- Download **JakeypooBWVRModInstaller.exe** from [the latest release](https://github.com/jmanoclay/BW-VR-Releases/releases/latest) and run it. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (it isn't code-signed yet): click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- The installer finds Black & White, brings it to v1.20 + Fan Patch 1.42 if needed (downloading the patches from bwgame.net with your OK), installs the mod, and has a **Launch in VR** button.
- Needs the English CD version of Black & White installed, Windows 10 or 11, and SteamVR. Developed and tested on a Valve Index.

## What's in it
- **Stereo 3D** of the whole game world at 90 Hz, with sound heard from your head.
- **The god hand is your hand:** pick up villagers, trees and rocks by touching them, throw at your hand's speed, catch things in flight, scoop from stores and fields, pet or slap your creature, hand it food.
- **The laser** (A) for reaching far: pick up, drag the land, double pull to travel.
- **Miracles:** hold B and draw the gesture in the air; pop miracle bubbles, pour miracles from your hand, circle the land for shields and storms.
- **Building:** carry a scaffold, see its blueprint below your hand, twist your wrist to turn it.
- **The leash:** walk your creature, send it to things, tie it up.
- **Moving:** stick to fly, snap turn, rise and descend, grab the world with both hands.
- **Cutscenes and menus** on a cinema screen you point at; the temple's rooms on that screen too.
- **A tuning panel** above your other hand (stick click) for throwing, grip, touch and more, saved to bwvr.ini.

## Controls in short
- **Trigger or grip:** pick up and carry. A quick trigger tap: interact.
- **A:** laser on/off. **B held:** draw a miracle gesture.
- **Other hand:** stick moves you; A opens the game's menu; stick click opens the tuning panel.
- **Ctrl+F12** on the keyboard recentres the view.
