## Fixed
- **Changing player** (Options > Players > Restart) no longer crashes the game.
- With **Main hand: left** in VR Options, each hand now shows the right hand model.

## New
- **VR from the start**: the game goes into VR by itself as soon as it starts, from the very first 3D frame. Launching it from the installer or straight from runblack.exe both work.
- **Creating a new player** opens the SteamVR keyboard to type the name.

## Installer 1.0.6
- **Launch in VR** just starts SteamVR and the game now; it no longer closes the menu or recentres the view for you.
- Updating from 0.1.0 or 0.2.0 switches on VR-from-the-start in your settings (those versions had it off). Nothing else in your settings changes, and the old file is kept in the backups.

## Installer 1.0.7
- Download **JakeypooBWVRModInstaller.exe** below; it keeps itself up to date.
- The window and Task Manager now show it as **Jakeypoo's Black & White VR Mod Installer**.
