## Improved
- **Things come to your hand at once**: villagers and trees are in your hand the moment you grip, instead of a beat later. A rock caught in the air no longer flies on first, and a walking villager no longer keeps you waiting.
- **Quick grabs count**: grab and throw in one motion, or grab and let go straight away, and it throws or drops as you meant. A quick grip is never a tap, so a rock no longer breaks.
- **Rocks sit steady in your palm** once held, with no catching up or jiggling.
- **Turning a building's blueprint**: turn your hand left or right (as seen from above) and the blueprint turns the same way, twice as far. It used to turn the wrong way most of the time.
- **Shake a disciple to release it**: shaking a villager in your hand works again; as in the flat game, it drops them and ends their disciple job.

## Fixed
- A tree pulled up no longer leaves its hole floating in the air.
- A thing let go of just as it reached your hand no longer blinks out for a moment.
- **Frozen creature**: the mod no longer cuts a petting press short, which is how both reported freezes started. If it ever happens again, loading your last save brings the creature back.

## Installer 1.0.14

## Known issues
- **Picking up rocks** can still take a moment after you grip.
- **"trigger - continue"** still shows on some lines that go away by themselves. Waiting works too.
- **Menu clicks** can now and then stop registering for a moment. Pointing away and back, or closing and reopening the menu, brings them back.
- **Menu tick sounds** may not play when the laser moves over the menu's buttons.
- **The hand looks see-through from behind** when you look down your wrist.
- **The Welcome to VR window** has small gaps in its top border.
- **Pouring food or wood from high up**: the stream doesn't quite reach your hand.
- **Small things in the distance** (sheep, fences) fade out a little early.
- **The gravity gloves' motes** can look slightly choppy.
- **A frozen creature**: rarely (seen twice), after petting it, the creature can stop responding. Loading your last save resets it.
