## New
- **Teleport movement** (VR Options → "Teleport with A"): hold **A** on your main hand to aim an energy arc at the land, let go to be there. You land at a comfortable height to reach the ground. With it on, A teleports instead of switching to the laser pointer; everything else stays the same.
- **Welcome to VR**: starting a new game in the headset, a short setup pops up first (turning, teleport, comfort vignette, main hand, move speed, recenter view). Continue takes you on to the game's own choices.
- **Comfort vignette** (VR Options: off / gentle / strong): the edges of the view darken while you move or turn smoothly. Off by default.
- **Recenter view** for seated play: on VR Options and the Welcome page, or hold the other hand's stick click for a second.
- **Gestures anywhere**: miracle gestures now work wherever you look, even at the sky, not only with land behind your hand.
- **The pointer laser** is now an energy beam, and the influence warning's stream flows from your hand.

## Improved
- **Autosave**: a "Saving..." panel shows in the middle of your view while the game saves, instead of the view just freezing.
- **The disciple icon** (the yellow figure over a villager you hold above a field, workshop or tree) is now big enough to see, just above the villager's head, and only shows for the villager in your hand or under it.
- **VR Options** are tidier: on/off settings are checkboxes, Recenter view is a button, the open tab looks selected. The water is always see-through.
- **Your hand fades** (both hands) outside your influence, where it can't act, as it used to.
- **"trigger - continue"** shows on fewer lines that go by themselves (the spirits' idle chatter no longer gets it).
- You can't fly about while one of the game's menus is up.
- The spirits now show on the cinema screen in the new game's opening.

## Known issues
- **Gesture shapes drift**: with your palm up toward the left of your view, the pink gesture shapes can drift away from their icons as you turn your head. The icons themselves stay in place.
- **"trigger - continue"** still shows on some lines that go away by themselves (some of the friendly god's lines). Waiting works too.
- **Menu clicks** can now and then stop registering for a moment, the cursor off from the laser. Pointing away and back, or closing and reopening the menu, brings it back.
- **Menu tick sounds** may not play when the laser moves over the menu's buttons.
- **The hand looks see-through from behind** when you look down your wrist.
- **The Welcome to VR window** has small gaps in its top border.
- **Teleport height**: you land low enough to pick things up, so the view is close to the ground. A higher, bird's-eye option with grabbing from afar is planned.
- **Autosave** still holds the view still for a moment while "Saving..." shows.
