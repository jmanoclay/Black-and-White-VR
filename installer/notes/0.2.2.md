## Fixed
- **Starting a new game** ("Start a New Game") and **changing player** no longer crash the game.
- **The coastlines**: the sea is now at its real depth.
- **Rivers** no longer show the reflected world through them.

## New
- **Choosing your creature** with the trigger: tap a creature once to pick it, tap it again to choose it.
- The opening splash, the intro videos and the loading screens now show **on a screen in the headset**. The trigger skips the splash.
- **Water** setting in VR Options: see-through (the default) or solid.

## Changed
- The tutorial's **camera lessons** (rotate, tilt, zoom) now complete when you turn, nod and move up and down in VR.
- The trigger **continues a caption** that stays on screen, and the "trigger - continue" prompt sits on the caption.
- **Story scrolls** next to trees can now be tapped.
- **Small objects** can be picked up wherever your hand is on them, including the tutorial's gate stone the first time, beside the field.

## Installer 1.0.8
- The **Controls** tab covers choosing your creature, captions, the splash and the Water setting.
