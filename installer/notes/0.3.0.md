## New
- **Gravity gloves**, as in Half-Life: Alyx: point an open hand at something out of reach (a villager, an animal, a rock, a tree, even something still flying through the air) and warm motes rise around it. Close your grip to lock on, then flick your hand back: it flies to your hand in an arc. Works from high above the village too. Only things in view and in your influence. VR Options: "Gravity gloves".
- **Both hands grab**: pick up, carry, throw and drop with either hand, and pass things from one hand to the other (with a buzz). Miracles, the laser and gestures stay on your main hand. VR Options: "The other hand grabs too".
- **Bird's-eye teleport**: teleport lands you high above the ground, like the flat game's view; reach down with the gravity gloves, or scoop from fields and stores below.
- **Choosing disciples**: carrying a grown villager, soft motes drift down to a pool of light where they'll land, in the colour of the job they'll take (farmer yellow, forester green, fisherman blue, builder orange, breeder pink). Disciples are made from village height; from high in the sky, villagers just fall.
- **Drop things from the sky**: let go of a villager, an animal, a tree or a scaffold high up and it falls. Near the ground they're still set down, planted or built where you let go.
- **Scoop from above**: from teleport height, reach down over a field, the store's food or its wood and grip to scoop. A light tick tells you when you're in reach.

## Improved
- **The sun, clouds and glows stay put** when you tilt or turn your head (the sun's glow used to wander and rotate with your head).
- **Gesture shapes stay under their icons** on your upturned left palm, and their preview dots no longer flicker.
- **Throwing feels right**: a hand that has stopped doesn't throw, so villagers set down gently stay put.
- **Trees planted** from the hand sink into the ground where you let go, instead of floating.
- **Picking up people** by buildings and stores is more reliable: people come before piles and fields, and a grip never sends your creature off on the leash.
- **Things held in the left hand** are the right way up.
- **The leash**: if a shake doesn't drop it, the mod drops it for you; a hand-over the creature doesn't take no longer leaves it waiting.

## Known issues
- **Picking things up takes a moment** (the game's own hand reaching for it), most noticeable when catching something in the air.
- **"trigger - continue"** still shows on some lines that go away by themselves. Waiting works too.
- **Menu clicks** can now and then stop registering for a moment. Pointing away and back, or closing and reopening the menu, brings them back.
- **Menu tick sounds** may not play when the laser moves over the menu's buttons.
- **The hand looks see-through from behind** when you look down your wrist.
- **The Welcome to VR window** has small gaps in its top border.
- **Pouring food or wood from high up**: the stream doesn't quite reach your hand.
- **Small things in the distance** (sheep, fences) fade out a little early.
- **The gravity gloves' motes** can look slightly choppy.
- **A frozen creature**: rarely, after petting it with the leash on, the creature can stop responding. Shaking now drops the leash; loading your last save resets the creature.
