## New
- **VR Options** in the game's pause menu (the other hand's A): snap or **smooth turning**, the snap angle (30 degrees to start), smooth turning speed, move speed, your main hand (left-handed play), the two-hand grab, and throw strength. Your choices are saved and kept through updates.
- **Smooth turning** with the main hand's stick, for those who prefer it to snaps.

## Changed
- **Lightning** now strikes from your fingertip.
- **Developer tools removed** from the player version: the menu's developer page (give yourself any miracle, the raw tuning settings) and the tuning panel on the other hand's stick click. Everyday settings are in VR Options instead.

## Installer 1.0.5
- The installer is now just **JakeypooBWVRModInstaller.exe**: no version in the name, since it updates itself. Download it from [the latest release](https://github.com/jmanoclay/BW-VR-Releases/releases/latest).
- The **Controls** tab is up to date with VR Options.
- With installer 1.0.4 or older, download **JakeypooBWVRModInstaller.exe** from [the latest release](https://github.com/jmanoclay/BW-VR-Releases/releases/latest) once; from then on it updates itself.
