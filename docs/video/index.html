Black & White VR Mod: Gameplay Video

Jakeypoo's alpha · Lionhead's Black & White (2001) in virtual reality · Watch on YouTube

This is Lionhead's Black & White (2001), played in a VR headset with Jakeypoo's free mod. Your hands are the hand of God: pick up villagers, animals, rocks and trees, throw them across the island, and catch them in the air.

The video walks through the game's menus on a floating screen, picking up and throwing, feeding the village, building with scaffolds, drawing miracle gestures in the air with your fingertip, hurling fireballs, and meeting your creature face to face: feeding it, petting it and training it.

The mod is free and works with your own PC copy of Black & White (2001) and a SteamVR headset. One installer patches the game, installs the mod and keeps it up to date.
